# Design Project 1 — AC-to-DC Power Supply

## Overview

Designed and built an AC-to-DC power supply targeting an output of **3.0 V ± 0.1 V at 10 mA** for ELECENG 2EI4: Electronic Devices and Circuits 1 at McMaster University.

The design uses a full-wave bridge rectifier and capacitor filter to convert a low-voltage AC input into a smoother DC output. The circuit was simulated in **LTspice**, then built and tested using the **Digilent Analog Discovery 3**.

## Results

The measured output ranged from **2.90 V to 3.10 V**, reaching the specified voltage limits under the tested conditions. The project explored component selection, ripple reduction, and differences between calculated, simulated, and measured performance.
