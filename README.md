# ELECENG-2EI4-Projects

This repository brings together my projects and reports for 2EI, a course focused on semiconductor devices and electronic circuit analysis and design.

## About the Course

2EI covers the electrical characteristics, operating principles, and circuit models of diodes, field-effect transistors, bipolar junction transistors (BJTs), and operational amplifiers. The course connects device behavior to the analysis and design of basic application circuits.

Topics include:

- **Device characterization:** interpreting current–voltage (I–V) characteristics, reading datasheets, and extracting diode, MOSFET, and BJT parameters.
- **DC analysis:** calculating large-signal currents and voltages in diode and transistor circuits.
- **Application circuits:** analyzing and designing circuits for level shifting, limiting, clamping, rectification, biasing, and digital logic.
- **Digital logic:** evaluating static and dynamic properties of NMOS and CMOS gates, and designing complex CMOS gates with appropriate transistor sizing.
- **Amplifiers:** performing small-signal analysis, designing single-stage MOSFET and BJT amplifiers, and representing amplifier behavior using two-port models.
